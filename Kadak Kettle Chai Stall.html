    Our story

    Three generations, one kettle

    Ramesh-ji started with a bicycle, a kerosene stove and one dented kettle outside the bus depot in 1998. His son added the bun maska. His granddaughter painted the sign.

    The kettle is still the same one. The chai is still boiled until it's kadak, and still poured from a height.
